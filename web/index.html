About

Every few hours this site reads public Harvard event calendars, plus any newsletters it is given, and keeps the events whose own description says food is served.

Food

Confirmed: one sentence names a food and says it is served (“A light lunch will be provided.”). That sentence is quoted on the card. Likely: only the title suggests food (“Opening Reception”). Online, brown-bag and pay-for-food events are left out.

Who may attend

Each event is checked for audience limits. The check reads the calendar's own audience field first (Harvard Law School lists “All HLS students only” or “All Harvard”; the College tags class years), then sentences such as “open to HUID holders only”, then clues such as “1L Coffee Chat” or a House as host.

  • Open to public anyone
  • Harvard ID Harvard ID holders: fellows qualify
  • Audience not stated
  • Probably only … inferred from the title or host
  • Only … limited to a school, class year, House or group

Going / Probably no access/food

Tap Going so other fellows see who is heading there, or Probably no access/food when you found out the event isn't open to us or there is no food after all. Votes are shared by everyone using this site and kept for 30 days. On the claude.ai copy, names come from Claude accounts, and voting needs Editor access; everyone else sees the votes.
